      Questions

      When do pre-orders close?

      Friday 9 October at 11:59pm NZ time. After that, this page closes and we start printing.

      When will I get my tees?

      Printing takes 2 to 4 days once pre-orders close. All orders will be shipped out or ready for pickup before the first game on Thursday 15 October.

      How does the multi-buy deal work?

      Order 2 or more tees in one order and every tee is $5 cheaper. Any mix counts: box fit, adult and kids, any colours and sizes. The saving is applied automatically.

      Can I order different sizes for the whole family?

      Yes. Add each tee with its own colour, size and quantity, then pay once for the whole order. Sorting a big church, club or team order? Email us at sales@labprints.co.nz.

      Where is the print?

      The 685 Samoa design is printed A3 size across the chest, with a small Samoa flag on the left sleeve.

      Which tee should I get?

      Go heavyweight box fit (Urban Collab, 280GSM) if you like a thick, boxy streetwear tee with a drop shoulder. The standard adult tee (Cloke Outline, 185GSM) is a lighter regular everyday fit.

      Size guide: Standard Adult Tee (Cloke Outline)

      Measurements are of the garment laid flat. Compare with a tee you already own that fits well.

      How do I pay?

      Card payment through Stripe secure checkout. You will get an email receipt straight away.

      Something wrong with my order?

      Email sales@labprints.co.nz with your receipt and we will sort it out.
