What is DTF?

The print tech that does it all.

DTF (Direct to Film) is the latest in garment printing — full colour, soft feel, works on almost any fabric, and no minimum orders required. It's replaced screen printing for most custom jobs.

✓
Works on cotton, polyester, nylon, and most fabric blends
✓
Full colour — no colour limits, no gradient restrictions
✓
Soft, flexible feel that moves with the garment
✓
Wash durable — holds up through regular laundering
✓
Apply yourself with a heat press or order finished garments
