FAQ

Common questions.

DTF (Direct to Film) transfers are custom prints on a special film that you heat press onto garments. They work on almost any fabric — cotton, polyester, nylon, blends — and produce vibrant, full-colour results with a soft feel.

A heat press gives the best results, but a household iron works too. Set to 150°C, press for 15–20 seconds, peel, and press again for 10 seconds. Full instructions included with every order.

There is no minimum. Order as few as one transfer or one garment. We cater to individuals, small businesses, and large-scale orders equally.

DTF transfers ship within 24 hours of order. Custom garments take 5–7 business days. We ship NZ-wide via NZ Post with tracking on every order.

PNG files with transparent backgrounds at 300 DPI minimum. If you’re unsure, send us what you have — we’ll optimise it for print at no extra charge.

Our DTF transfers are rated for 100+ washes. They stretch and rebound without cracking, fading, or peeling. Machine wash inside-out on cold for best results.
